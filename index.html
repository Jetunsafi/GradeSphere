About ERMS

Exam Result Management System (ERMS) is a comprehensive web-based solution designed to streamline the process of result publication and access. It eliminates the traditional method of result notice boards and provides instant online access to examination results.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I check my result?

Simply click on "Check Result" button, enter your roll number and select your course to view your result instantly.

Can I download my result?

Yes, after viewing your result, you can download it as PDF or take a printout directly from the result page.

What if I forget my password?

Contact your class teacher or admin office to reset your password. They will provide you with new credentials.

Is my result secure?

Yes, all results are password protected and only accessible with valid roll number and password.

When are results published?

Results are typically published within 15-20 days after the examination. You'll receive a notification once they're available.

Can I get a physical copy of my marksheet?

Yes, you can collect your original marksheet from the college administration office after the results are declared.
